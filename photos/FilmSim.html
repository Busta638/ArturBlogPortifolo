Behind the roll

Photography

Photography is a hobby that gives me a way to document the colors, details, and atmosphere of the world around me. I am especially drawn to street scenes, cars, motorsports, and architecture, while also exploring sports, portraits, nature, and wildlife.

Color and light shape the mood of every image I edit. Sometimes that mood is cinematic or dramatic; other times it is quiet, nostalgic, or natural. I focus on what catches my attention in the moment and use editing to strengthen the feeling that was already there.

Every frame in the film roll includes a date, turning the gallery into a timeline of my work. It shows how my photography has changed, where I have improved, and even the days when the results were not as strong. Those photographs still belong in the story because progress is easier to understand when the imperfect moments are included too.

This portfolio is not meant to present perfection. It is a collection of work I have chosen to put into the world because it represents how I see my surroundings and what I am capable of creating. Photography remains something I do as a hobby, and I am proud to share the process as much as the finished images.
